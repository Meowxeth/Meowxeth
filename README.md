## Featured projects

### Sheilz POS and IMS (Capstone)

A point-of-sale and inventory system that ran a live coffee shop's daily operations for over a month. It pairs an offline-first mobile POS with a web admin and business intelligence dashboard, both on Supabase. My part covered the mobile-to-database transaction flow, the loyalty portal (30+ active customers), the offline-to-cloud sync, and the PostgreSQL schema, migrations, and RLS policies. Recipe-based inventory deduction runs automatically through Postgres functions and triggers, and PayMongo payments are verified with HMAC-SHA256 webhooks in Supabase Edge Functions.

`React Native` `Expo` `SQLite` `Zustand` `Next.js` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `PayMongo`

[Mobile POS](https://github.com/ChrisQuint0/Sheilz-POS-Mobile) · [Web admin dashboard](https://github.com/ChrisQuint0/Sheilz-POS-Web)

### P.A.C.E. (Pasig Alumni Career and Employability System)

A platform that gives alumni curated career opportunities and gives the institution employability analytics. I built the ML backend: dual Random Forest employability models and salary and job-search duration regression models on 40+ academic and skills features, with feature-importance analysis and prediction confidence intervals. Job recommendations use 384-dimensional Sentence Transformer embeddings with GPU acceleration, and ARIMA(1,1,1) forecasting with Ljung-Box diagnostics supports workforce insights.

`Python` `FastAPI` `scikit-learn` `Sentence Transformers` `Next.js` `Supabase` `Docker`

[Source code](https://github.com/KlyrhonMiko/pace)

### MENACE in Hexapawn

An implementation of the Machine Educable Noughts and Crosses Engine (MENACE) applied to Hexapawn, a learning system built from matchboxes and beads, translated to code. Originally a high school research project, inspired by Vsauce2.
